# About these notes

This document serves as a collection of notes and ideas related to the research project on this protocol. Some may be may be more polished than others, some are brainfarts and simply about the things I want to remember, reflections of and actual dialogues with myself. Unfiltered auto-ethnography of the research process itself in some way.

Many of these notes were originally written in Obsidian, on paper, or on apple notes on my phone. Many are just notes of me speaking to myself and using voice-to-text transcription on my computer, then sometimes editing for sense. When I speak to myself, a re-parsing through reading and typing occurs.

All have been ported here for better organisation and to be part of the research archive as a process I started while drafting the Springer chapter on the topic.

These notes are a work in progress and will continue to be updated and expanded as the research project evolves.
