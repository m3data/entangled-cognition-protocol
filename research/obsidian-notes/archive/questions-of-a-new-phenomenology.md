# Questions of a New Phenomenology

- How can phenomenology evolve to better account for the entanglement of human and machine cognition in contemporary experiences? Varela's enactive approach offers a starting point, but what new frameworks are needed? What about Thompson's work on lifeworlds and technogenesis? Cybernetics?
- In what ways can we reconceptualise the lifeworld to include AI as a co-constitutive element of experience? How does this shift our understanding of embodiment, perception, and intersubjectivity?
- Is this even needed? Or is it just an extension of existing phenomenological methods?
- How can we methodologically approach the study of human-AI interactions phenomenologically? What new tools or techniques are required to capture the nuances of these interactions?
- What ethical considerations arise when integrating AI into phenomenological research? How do we navigate issues of agency, consent, and representation in human-AI contexts?
- How can a new phenomenology inform the design and development of AI systems to better align with human values and experiences? Can phenomenological insights lead to more empathetic and responsive AI?
- What role does moral imagination play in shaping our interactions with AI? How can phenomenology help us understand and cultivate this imagination in ethical world-building?
- What of participatory sense-making practices from Di Paolo and De Jaegher? How can these be adapted or extended to include AI as active participants in meaning-making processes?
- How can we foster interdisciplinary collaboration between phenomenologists, AI researchers, ethicists, artists, indigenous knowledge holders, and others to co-create a new phenomenology that is responsive to the challenges and opportunities of human-AI entanglement in the age of great unraveling that Joanna Macy speaks of?
- How can we ensure that this new phenomenology remains grounded in lived experience while also engaging with the technical complexities of AI? What balance needs to be struck between theory and practice? Mimicry vs provable coherence and cognitive coupling?
- How might the entangled cognition protocol serve as a practical framework for exploring these questions? What insights can be gleaned from its application in real-world human-AI interactions?

Oh my, just writing these questions is fucking daunting. Where to even start?
