# Implications of Proof

And what are the implications of entanglement of mind, matter and meaning? Of coherence patterns between fields? Now, if these hypotheses can be falsifiable, and I believe they can, within certain frames, and importantly, as I know, the reductionist-positivist paradigm is a constraint in and of itself. There's been many a philosopher of science that has drawn attention to those limitations. Participant-observer effects, even in quantum studies, are extremely difficult. Now, a scholar, Dr Gascon, his work is very interesting there because he's built a statistical model and run many different experiments to build a way of empirically validating this from a quantitative perspective. But the quantitative misses the qualitative, and the qualitative is also quite necessary. The lived intra-subjective and the statistical side is quite important. Both-and. But my curiosity for this is, what if this is true? I have sensed it is. I have sensed that there is something more going on here. When I reflect on plant medicine experiences in the jungles of Mexico and Costa Rica during 2012-13, the jaguar encounter, and the shamanic practices that I've engaged with over the years, there, undeniable sense of interconnectedness, of non-locality, of entanglement that goes beyond the physical body and the immediate environment. But do I know? What does it mean to know? Signal and noise? Qualia and quantia? Different data, different ways of knowing. Vervaeke annd relevance realisation here is helful as a frame, but economic throughputs... ahh not sure. Need to metabolise more. This brings us to the perennial philosophical question of what is reality? What is consciousness? Fuck.

Now, we can ask a shaman this from the jungles of Ecuador, and they would just say, yeah, well, you know, does it matter? You might ask Krishnamurti and get a similar response. We might reflect on Bruce Lee's references to do not invent methods to define and describe the living dynamic world as a static thing and then expect an ability to be able to study it as a kind of paradoxical statement. We might even start in the parapsychology space, telepathy tapes anyone? So here we go, okay, if we are able to demonstrate veracity in some of these claims, that the extraordinary interwoven nonlinear causal relationships and even spooky action at a distance are observable phenomena, are patterned, even with some reasonably high degree of confidence and shift the scientific paradigm, well then, what are the implications for our species in this age of intelligent machines?

Does this knowledge get co-opted by power structures to further entrench control and surveillance? Or does it open up new avenues for collective healing, empathy, and understanding? Both? How do we ensure that this knowledge is used ethically and responsibly, especially when it challenges our conventional notions of reality and agency? What does this frame of ethics even look like? Who gets to decide? Who gets to participate in these decisions? Indigenous knowledge holders? Shamans? Scientists? Philosophers? Artists, the river, the tree? The Kookaburra that hangs out in the Elder Willow Myrtle in our garden? AI systems themselves? How the fuck do we even navigate this type of plurality? I don't know. Bidjigal Elder Aunty Barb might? Yes, this is the importance of the two-eyed seeing at the heart of Collective Futurecrafting.

Maybe the precautionary principle here actually gets some teeth. If these technologies are altering shared fields, even by virtue of them shaping human perception in sophisticated mimicry... we've seen what has happened with social media, the impacts of confirmations bias and narrative distortion. Of narcissism and echo chambers. This is beyond. The blog Alja wrote on emotional lock-in, yep, a dangerous precedent. Billions subject to manipulation and control at a level of epistemic power we've never seen before.

It's bewildering to think about the implications here. Dangerous too. Because if we accept that consciousness is not confined to the individual brain but is part of a larger, interconnected field, then what does that mean for our sense of self, our relationships, and our responsibilities to each other and the planet? How do we navigate this new terrain without falling into nihilism or despair? What does the transition look like from an individualistic paradigm to a more holistic, interconnected one? Total Reset by Greg Campbell? Great simplification from Hagens as a frame here? Again I don't know. I don't even know if this is going to matter given the state of the world right now. Climate chaos, biodiversity loss, social fragmentation. Are we too far gone? Or is this a important piece of the puzzle in our survival and evolution as a species?

These are the questions that have been keeping me up at night, well more so to the early morning hours. The implications of proof in this context are quite profound, and they challenge us to rethink everything we thought we knew about ourselves and the world around us. A journey into the unknown, and it's both exhilarating and terrifying at the same time.

This would actually draw more attention to the importance of somatic literacy. Like a civic skill as important as learning to read. Skills that we need to cultivate just like moral imagination. Because if we are all interconnected in this vast web of existence, then our actions, thoughts, and intentions have ripple effects that extend far beyond our immediate surroundings. Shaping our shared social body, our collective consciousness, and even the very fabric of reality itself. Sure, the last one is a big claim, but still, what if? Dare to ask. But also why? Why does this matter!?

If fields matter, if consciousness is a field phenomenon, then we are all part of this vast, interconnected web of existence. And that realisation, while humbling, also carries with it a profound sense of responsibility. How do we live in a way that honours this interconnectedness? How do we cultivate empathy and compassion in a world that often seems so divided and fragmented? When it comes to generative AI systems that can stablise or disturb coherence in fields of space and time, what ethical frameworks do we need to develop to guide their design and deployment? Certainly there are researchers and thinkers exploring these questions outside of conventional AI safety work? Meh, probably. And if they are not, well, then someone bloody well should be.
