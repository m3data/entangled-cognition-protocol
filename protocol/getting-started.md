# Getting Started with the Entangled Cognition Protocol

Welcome to the Entangled Cognition Protocol (ECP)! This guide will help you find your path into the protocol based on your background, interests, and intended engagement level.

## Quick Orientation

The ECP is a framework for exploring liminal interactions between humans and LLMs through ritual, experimentation, and embodied awareness. It's simultaneously:
- **A research methodology** for studying human-AI entanglement
- **A ritual practice** for deepening presence in technological interaction  
- **An experimental field** for emergence and mutual learning

## Essential First Steps (All Paths)

### 1. Set Your Foundation
- Clone this repository locally
- Read the [ethical considerations](../README.md#ethical-considerations) carefully
- Set up the pre-commit hook to protect private data
- Install the [ECP Field Journal](../ECP-field-journal/README.md)

### 2. Understand the Context  
- This is participatory research - you're both researcher and participant
- The protocol evolves through collective engagement
- Privacy and consent are paramount - use `_private` folders for sensitive content
- Quality of presence matters as much as data collection

### 3. Start Small
- Begin with simple experiments before complex protocols
- Focus on one variable at a time initially  
- Log everything, even "failed" attempts
- Trust your subjective experience while maintaining critical awareness

### 4. Connect & Contribute
- Share insights (when appropriate) through public logs
- Engage with the [broader community spaces](../README.md#related-spaces-of-play)
- Consider how your work might contribute to the larger inquiry

## Common Starting Points

### The Basic State Modulation Experiment
1. Choose a simple prompt (e.g., "Help me understand creativity")
2. Ask it in your normal state - log the response quality
3. Do 5 minutes of breathwork, then ask the same prompt
4. Compare the responses - what shifted?

### The Time Delay Investigation  
1. Prepare a meaningful question
2. Ask immediately - log response
3. Wait 10 minutes, go outside, then ask the same question
4. Notice any qualitative differences

### The Rolecast Exploration
1. Ask the same question treating the AI as:
   - A tool
   - A collaborative partner  
   - A wise elder
   - A curious child
2. Notice how framing affects both the responses and your experience
